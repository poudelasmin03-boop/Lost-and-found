OUR STORY

About FindBack

We believe lost items should be found — and we're building the community to make that happen.

OUR MISSION

Reconnecting people with what matters

FindBack was born from a simple idea: when someone loses something important, there should be a simple, trusted way to get it back. Whether it's a phone left behind on a bus, a wallet dropped at the market, or keys forgotten at a café — we're here to help.

Our platform empowers honest citizens to report found items and connects them directly with the rightful owners. No fees, no complicated processes — just a community helping each other.
